## Me Blog
I’ve started Green Blue Security to share my journey in cybersecurity (for more on this please read [Introduction]({% post_url 2026-10-20-introduction %})). Here I will publish original Information Security analysis and research covering Current Events, Data Privacy, Grassroots & Open-Source Technology, Information Gathering, Mobile Security and a lot of personal opinions. I know cybersecurity is universally and vitally important; Jackie Singh explained my reasons better than I ever could:
> I treat cybersecurity not as an abstract engineering discipline, but as critical infrastructure for democratic participation, personal safety, and institutional accountability. - Singh via [Hacking, but Legal](https://www.hackingbutlegal.com/)

For my purposes, I will focus on 1) Personal Safety to support 2) Democratic Participation in order to establish 3) Institutional Accountability. It is the peoples responsibility to hold institutions accountable; institutional accountability has never happened on its own.

  **Disclaimer:**
  If you ask me which way I lean, I would say generally toward the AI Doomer. Though I can imagine quite a few benevolent applications, I can't imagine how many of them would ever get implemented given current leadership. I will never use LLMs to write my words; all of these posts will be directly from me. However, I have and will use LLMs to help me develop and maintain the web site infrastructure. Things like my CSS styles sheet and navigation tools.
