## Brief
 I want to provide some insight on why I joined the cybersecurity profession and started my own blog. I have always been fascinated and maybe a lil entranced by technology. I did feel like a true grasp of the technology was out of my reach assuming it was a field for people much smarter than I. And that is, but there is also plenty of room for people like and unlike me.

## Work
 This world is entangled in a world wide web of IT infrastructure. And as much as we like to draw dividing lines, it represents one of the most incredible collaborative efforts in human history. Across counties, states, countries, continents and into outerspace (i.e. Hubble, James Webb, etc.) And this weave is a reflection/amalgamation/distillation of it’s creators... It’s a chaotic, beautiful, sadistic mess. Though, I usually feel like technology amplifies everything, these days there appears to be a bias in the system for the sadistic, the insensitive, or maybe the self absorbed. I don’t know if it’s by design, but I do believe either way that it is an eventuality given the predisposition of the few tech companies who oversee it. We thought this would level the playing field, and I still think it can. But, disruption, upheaval, rebellion always creates a power vacuum. And that has been seized by those with the cruelty required to subjugate their own race, their own planet (ref: [Enshittification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enshittification) | [Cory Doctorow](https://craphound.com/bio/)).
